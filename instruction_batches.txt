This is a quick instructional video for how to use Watch Together with Robo KJ.
----
When you first visit the link to Robo KJ's Watch Together session,
you do not need to log in. You can opt out of everything.
Then you'll be asked to pick a nickname.
----
The first thing you will want to do when you join the room
is to open the quote bubbles so that you can type commands to Robo KJ.
----
When you first enter the room, you are not registered. First command is to register a stage name. It is
best to use the same name of your user in the Zoom window. To register, you type the command
slash register and follow it with your singer name.
----
I'm going to put in Chad.
----
Robo KJ tells me that Chad has been added to the roster. At this point, you can make requests the way you would
normally make requests for videos in watch together. So, I have some links that I have already validated.
----
When I paste those into the chat window in watch together, Robo KJ tells me that a request has been added to the
queue for Chad.
----
I'm going to add a couple more. You can only put in a maximum of five at a time.
----
Robo KJ will start your video when it's your turn to sing.
----
You can use the Zoom window, unmute your microphone, and sing along with the song. Always remember to mute your
microphone in Zoom when you've finished singing your song.
----
There are some additional commands that I can give to Robo KJ.
The slash Q command will show songs that are lined up in my Q, Chad's Q.
----
There's a history command. Slash h i s t o r y.
----
If I wanted to delete all of the songs remaining in my queue because I've changed my mind, there's a delete Q command.
Slash d e l q.
----
I can ask for a restart for the video with slash r e s t a r t. This will only succeed in the first 30 seconds
of your video.
----
To avoid hogging the stage by any one user, the video will be timed out if it's longer than 4 minutes and 30
seconds. Check your videos beforehand so that you don't get an unpleasant surprise.
----
If Robo KJ encounters the dreaded extension needed screen, it becomes the next singer's turn. The singer with the
faulty video URL will be bumped down the roster. If there is another song in that singer's request
queue, it will play.
----
Robo KJ will send
messages at the end of each video to let you know who is on stage, who is up next, and who is after that.
----
Muting and unmuting in the Zoom session is the responsibility of the singers. Take care that your
microphone isn't ruining another singer's performance.
----
Use the chat window or your own microphone to remind others when their microphone should be muted.
----
Be kind and humble. We all make mistakes. Okay, enjoy your show.
